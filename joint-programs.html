申请前，了解这几件事Before you apply

费用包含什么？What do the fees cover?

所列为对应简章年份的国内年度学费参考，不是完整留学预算。住宿、注册、教材、境外学费及生活费等需另行核算。Figures refer to annual domestic tuition in the stated brochure year, not the total study-abroad cost. Budget separately for accommodation, registration, materials, overseas tuition and living expenses.

如何选择海外学校？How is the overseas university chosen?

根据专业匹配、课程成绩、英语要求和当年接收名单确定。录取、学位授予及学历认证须分别满足相应条件。Options depend on subject fit, course results, English requirements and the current receiving-university list. Admission, degree awards and qualification recognition each have their own conditions.
